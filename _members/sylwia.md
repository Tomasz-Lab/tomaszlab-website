Sylwia holds a Bachelor’s and Master’s degree in Bioinformatics at the Jagiellonian University. Her Master’s thesis focused on the quantitative analysis of endogenous single-strand DNA breaks in mouse somatic and germ cells.
In years 2023 – 2025, Sylwia served as the president of the Association of Bioinformatics Students In Silico and was the main organizer of the BioMeeting 2025 Conference.
She is also the co-founder of the Bioinformatics Community PL organization, where she actively organizes meet-ups and other initiatives for bioinformatics professionals and enthusiasts eager to share knowledge, experience, and ideas about the future of this rapidly evolving field. Her main research interests are human microbiome studies and its impact on health and disease.
