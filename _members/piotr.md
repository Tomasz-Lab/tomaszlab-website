Piotr is a computer scientist and data scientist with an interdisciplinary background in cognitive science and philosophy (PhD). At Sano, he supports the microbiomeGPT project by developing tools for metagenomic data processing, model training and evaluation. His research interests include computational intelligence, optimization methods, and the interdisciplinary (primarily logical) aspects of negation.
